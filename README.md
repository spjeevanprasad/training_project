# TaskSprint PRO 🌧️ — Dim Rainy Window & Transparent Glass UI

A high-performance sprint task management application featuring a **Node.js + Express** REST API backend and a **React (Vite)** frontend with an **atmospheric dim rainy window backdrop** and **ultra-clear transparent glassmorphism buttons & forms**.

---

## 🌧️ Dim Rainy Window & Rain On Glass Engine

1. **Exact Dim Atmospheric Backdrop**:
   - Features the moody overcast foggy city skyline photograph seen through a rain-splattered window pane.
   - Tinted with a deep, dim moody color overlay (`#080d16` to `#182232`) for an authentic rainy day aesthetic.

2. **Dynamic Rain-on-Glass Physics Engine**:
   - **Trickling Droplets**: Water droplets gather mass, pause, and slide down the window pane in realistic meandering paths, leaving wet trails that gradually dry.
   - **Static Condensation Beads**: Micro-droplets resting on the glass with refractive highlights and meniscus drop shadows.
   - **Background Rain Streaks**: High-speed rain cutting through the foggy atmosphere outside the window.
   - **Interactive Water Physics**: Moving your mouse or clicking across the screen leaves water beads and streaks across the glass.
   - **Rain Controller**: A **"Rain 🌧️ / Dry"** toggle in the header allows pausing or resuming the active rainfall.

---

## 💎 Ultra-Clear Transparent Glass UI

1. **Transparent Glass Buttons & Form**:
   - The form, inputs, and buttons are crafted from **ultra-clear transparent glass** (`background: rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05 - 0.12)`) with a specular top light refraction (`inset 0 1px 1.5px rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.35)`).
   - You can see the falling rain and sliding droplets directly through the form and buttons!
   - High-contrast white typography (`#ffffff` / `#f8fafc`) with subtle drop shadow ensures readability against any rain background.

2. **Transparent Glass Capsules**:
   - **Task Input**: Translucent capsule (`border-radius: 9999px`) with gentle glass glow on focus.
   - **Dropdowns**: Transparent capsules with subtle white glass borders.
   - **Add Task Button**: Translucent glass action pill with tactile click bounce (`transform: scale(0.97)`).
   - **Task Cards**: Translucent glass panes floating on the window.
   - **Segmented Controls**: iOS-style transparent capsule tracks with frosted active indicator pills.
